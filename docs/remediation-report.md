## 1. Executive Summary

This report documents the modular remediation applied to `cristianarando.dev` across all five supported locales (`en`, `es`, `de`, `ru`, `ja`), following the audit specification while honoring every explicit owner override and exclusion.

### Key Highlights

- **Security & Edge Routing:** Removed `'unsafe-inline'` from `script-src` in `vercel.json` (preserving `'unsafe-inline'` only in `style-src`) and added `og` and `404` to `RESERVED_ROOT_SEGMENTS` and catch-all rewrite negative lookaheads, enforced by automated unit and post-build checks. Kept `SEC-02` (reverse proxy to `inside-link`), `SEC-04` (`img-src https:`), and `SEC-05` (`http://atlas.umss.edu.bo`) intentionally unchanged per owner confirmation.
- **SEO & Structured Data:** Unified professional title to **"Software & Systems Engineer"** across `SITE`, `site.webmanifest`, `og-template.ts`, `de/home.ts`, and Schema.org `Person`; added explicit `category` frontmatter (`technology`, `security`, `philosophy`, `literature`) to all 40 blog posts across 5 locales and localized `articleSection` resolution; completed 2-/3-level `BreadcrumbList` schemas for project case studies and discovery hubs; removed invalid `<meta http-equiv="last-modified">`; and added build-time Open Graph PNG (`1200×630`) inspection.
- **Search, Filtering & Maintainability:** Extracted pure list helpers (`filterItems`, `sortItems`, `paginateItems`, `scoreItem`, `isInvertedDateRange`, `swapViewClasses`, `validateSearchEntries`, `getListViewClasses`) into `src/utils/list.ts`; eliminated duplicate controls between `BlogManager.astro` and `ProjectsManager.astro` via shared `ListControls.astro` and `ListFooter.astro`; aligned rolling date range labels (`Last 7/30/365 days`) with inverted-range validation UI; indexed all 7 project case studies into `/search-index.json`; calibrated Japanese (`ja`) reading time; and upgraded `pnpm lint` to run both Prettier formatting (`format:check`) and strict TypeScript unused/return checks (`tsc --noEmit`).
- **Content, Academic Accuracy & UX:** Corrected `"Diplomat"` to `"Postgraduate Diploma in Information and Technology Security"` and documented the dual-degree (_doble titulación_) graduation modality; repaired the corrupted English paste in `de/my-final-project.mdx`; specified **OWASP Top 10:2021** and **OWASP API Security Top 10 (2023)** with a point-in-time (`2024`) disclaimer and accurate UMSS presentation `imageAlt`; moved `"HTML"` and `"CSS"` out of **Languages** into **Frontend** across all 5 locales (`TXT-08`); updated the Hero CV CTA to **"View CV"** (`file-text.svg`); preserved original About/Hero body typography size per owner visual review; removed Certifications nested scroll trap; added progressive tag disclosure in `BlogManager.astro`; and regenerated `public/screenshot.webp` (`1903×955`).
